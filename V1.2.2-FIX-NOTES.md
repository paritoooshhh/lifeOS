# LifeOS Midnight v1.2.2 — AI Planning and Daily Metrics

This is a frontend-only patch based on v1.2.1. It does not change Clerk, Cloudflare Worker, D1 schema, or cloud sync.

- AI planning prompts now provide explicit instructions to generate a complete proposed schedule instead of a single literal activity. Generic "Plan my day" activities are filtered from the review screen.
- Proposed activities from planning prompts default to `planned` with no actual duration.
- AI review now supports an optional `endDate` and prevents saving timed activities with a missing end time.
- Home and planner show activities spanning the selected date, including overnight sessions.
- Daily planned duration is calculated using actual overlap with the selected local calendar day.
- Time & attention shows *planned category hours* separately from *actual recorded category hours*.
- The service worker cache key was bumped.

## Important

AI model inference still runs through your existing deployed Worker. The frontend adds planning instructions to the request but cannot guarantee a full sensible schedule in every response. For a more capable AI with user preferences and true calendar-aware replanning, the authenticated Worker should be upgraded separately after inspecting its current deployed source.

Planned activities are not actual logs. Completed status does not imply a confirmed actual duration. Existing incorrectly completed activities are not silently rewritten.

## Smoke tests

1. Ask "Plan my day. I woke up at 8:30". Verify the review has multiple meaningful proposals and none called "Plan my day". If the AI cannot produce valid entries, it should not save a dummy plan.
2. Plan Nov 19, 21:00 to Nov 20, 02:00. Check Nov 19 planned=3h and Nov 20 planned=2h; actual=0h until confirmed.
3. On Nov 20, check the overnight session appears in Home timeline and planner.
4. Check AI-generated activities are planned, not completed, and review/edit before saving.
5. Confirm Clerk sign-in, cloud sync, and iPhone cache refresh.
